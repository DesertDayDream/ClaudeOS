How to read these results

Quality gates come first. The optional web task uses behaviour tests (65), robustness checks (10) and a blind appearance vote (25). Your vote replaces the other-family AI judge's appearance score. These weights are provisional.

Custom prompts have no automatic quality score. Fresh, cached and output tokens are reported separately; these are usage measurements, not subscription charges. Cached input is not evidence that research was avoided.

Accounting covers the matched session and native Claude subagents. Separate employee, coaching and judge sessions are excluded. This version does not measure the full cost of a multi-session team job. Match the other app's settings manually.
